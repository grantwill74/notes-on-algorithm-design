# ME 6: Graph modelling

You are moving through a tile maze from source (`S`) to destination (`D`). Walls (`=`) are impassable. You have $h$ health. If, after moving, you are adjacent to a monster (`M`), you take 1 damage. A monster is impassable like a wall. You may only move in the 4 cardinal directions. A move costs 1 time unit. A sample map looks like this:
```
============
=S      = D=
=    M  M  =
=M         =
============
```

The goal is to determine the shortest number of time units it takes to reach `D`. 

1. (25 pts) Define how you encode a minimal state to be put in the priority queue for Dijkstra's algorithm or A\*. That is, show me what variables go in the state. Include the metric in the state.

2. (25 pts) Given a state, how do we derive valid neighboring states? You can show me how every valid neighboring state for one cardinal direction is derived, and then say "other directions encoded similarly".

3. (10 pts) What is the best choice of heuristic that could be used to guide an A\* implementation of this problem. It should not consider health, only distance to goal.

4. (40 pts) Prove that your choice is consistent.
