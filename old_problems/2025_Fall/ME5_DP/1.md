# ME 5: DP

Suppose there is a frog named "Jumpy" (short for Jumpothy). Jumpy is at the bottom of $n$ stairs, $n \ge 1$. He can jump 1 stair or 2. There are at most 1000 stairs.

- If there were 1 stair, he can jump it. One way to get to the top.
- If there were 2 stairs, he could jump each, or jump both. 2 ways to get to the top.
- If 3 stairs, he could jump 1 + 1 + 1, or 2 + 1, or 1 + 2. 3 ways
- If 4 stairs, he could jump 2 + 2, or 2 + 1 + 1, or 1 + 2 + 1, or 1 + 1 + 2, or 1 * 5. 5 ways.

Help Jumpy know how many ways he can climb the stairs by defining this function (100 pts):
```c
int n_ways(int n);
```

Your solution must run in $O(n)$ to receive any credit at all.
You may not use any outside functions to receive any credit at all.

Hint: try writing out some terms of the sequence as the number of stairs increases. Alternatively, is there anything inductive you notice? Like knowing the solution for 2 and 3 stairs helps us get the solution to 4 stairs?
